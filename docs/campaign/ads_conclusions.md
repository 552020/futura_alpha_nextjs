* **Transcendence**: Generated moderate traffic but the highest cost per visit, indicating weaker conversion efficiency compared to other narratives.

### Conclusion

The campaign shows clear performance differentiation by narrative and format. Among video ads, the **wedding vertical** delivers the strongest balance between reach and conversion efficiency, while **family** and **transcendence** underperform in driving intent. The **static image (kiss)** significantly outperforms all reels in cost per visit and visit rate, indicating that simpler, emotionally explicit creatives are currently more effective for traffic acquisition. Future iterations should prioritize static formats and wedding-related messaging, while reassessing the role and positioning of abstract or broad narratives.
