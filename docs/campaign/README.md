# Campaign Analysis

This folder contains a comprehensive analysis of the campaign performance across four different ad creatives. The analysis compares video reels (Transcendence, Wedding, Family) with a static image (Kiss) to evaluate their effectiveness in driving website visits.

The analysis uses two separate sources of metrics: **Instagram/Meta ad platform metrics** (views, spend, website visits, cost per visit) and **Juno Analytics metrics** (visitor sessions, page views, bounce rate) that track on-site behavior. These are presented in separate sections below.

### Instagram/Meta One-line Takeaway per Vertical

- **Transcendence**: Generated moderate traffic but the highest cost per visit, indicating weaker conversion efficiency compared to other narratives.
- **Wedding**: Best-performing video format, combining the highest visit rate among reels with strong cost efficiency.
- **Family**: Achieved the widest reach but translated poorly into website visits, suggesting low intent despite broad appeal.
- **Kiss (static image)**: Outperformed all other ads in cost efficiency and visit rate, confirming static imagery with a warm, emotional cue as the strongest driver of traffic.

### Instagram/Meta Conclusion

The campaign shows clear performance differentiation by narrative and format. Among video ads, the **wedding vertical** delivers the strongest balance between reach and conversion efficiency, while **family** and **transcendence** underperform in driving intent. The **static image (kiss)** significantly outperforms all reels in cost per visit and visit rate, indicating that simpler, emotionally explicit creatives are currently more effective for traffic acquisition. Future iterations should prioritize static formats and wedding-related messaging, while reassessing the role and positioning of abstract or broad narratives.
