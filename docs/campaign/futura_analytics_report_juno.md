## Key Insights

1. **Mobile-First Audience:** Over 91% of visitors access the site via mobile devices, with iOS slightly leading Android.

2. **Instagram-Driven Traffic:** Instagram is the dominant traffic source, accounting for 85 direct referrals and 194 campaign-attributed visits.

3. **European User Base:** The majority of visitors are from European time zones, particularly Italy (Rome) and Germany (Berlin).

4. **High Bounce Rate:** At 97%, the bounce rate indicates most visitors leave after viewing just one page (average 1.04 pages per session).

5. **Content Focus:** The /en (main), /en/family, and /en/wedding pages are the most popular content sections.
